Admission Process

Four simple steps.

01

Enquiry

Fill the enquiry form or contact our office directly by phone.

02

Application

Submit the admission form along with the required documents.

03

Verification

Document verification and a short interaction with faculty.

04

Confirmation

Admission confirmation and fee submission to secure the seat.

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate
  • Mark Sheet (Previous Class)
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Passport Size Photo
  • Aadhaar Card (if available)
