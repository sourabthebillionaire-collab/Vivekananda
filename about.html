Our Legacy, Our Mission

Built on Swamiji's own words.

Vivekananda Higher Secondary School & +2 Junior Science College (Residential), Malkangiri, has been raised as a center of disciplined, values-first education — the school's own motto is painted right across its Main Road gate: build your life with Swamiji's ideals and inspiration.

We believe in the holistic development of students through quality education, discipline and a safe residential environment where every child is known, not just enrolled.

  • Quality education for all
  • Discipline and moral values
  • Holistic, well-rounded development
  • Safe, supervised residential campus
